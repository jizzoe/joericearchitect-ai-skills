## Source Quality Notes

- AWS documentation and program pages are authoritative for AWS account,
  security, and program claims.
- TechSoup is the AWS program distribution partner and is authoritative for its
  qualification and checkout flow.
- Current official pages conflict on promotional-credit amounts and offer
  mechanics. The findings intentionally do not select an amount; verify the
  active TechSoup offer at application time.
